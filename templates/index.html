Transaction Counts & Rounding

Debit transactions are randomly selected at 45–70% of the credit count. At least 13 customer transactions are required.

10–20% of debit transactions have amounts above 50,000; 20–30% of credit transactions have amounts below 30,000. Debit ordering includes random pairs and single transactions. Interest, tax, and balance rows are excluded.

Automatic: 70% in multiples of 1,000 or 500; 20% in multiples of 100 or 50; 10% in multiples of 5.

Enter percentages for the combined number of debit and credit transactions. Figures are assigned randomly across both columns. Interest, tax, and balance rows are excluded.

Total: 100%

Each amount is counted in its largest matching rounding figure, so categories do not overlap. Percentages are converted to the nearest whole transaction counts.
